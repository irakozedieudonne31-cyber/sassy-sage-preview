Before a real launch

Owner setup still needed

Private services and accurate business information must be supplied and approved before the live shop can work.

Secure backend & services

  • Configure the secure backend for products, accounts, and orders
  • Set up and test an SMS provider for one-time codes; provide a clear email alternative if available
  • Choose and configure an approved secure payment provider
  • Keep secret keys on the server, never in front-end code
  • Test permissions, backups, errors, and order flows before launch

Business details to confirm

  • Real catalog, product photos, names, descriptions, variants, dimensions, materials and care
  • Currency, prices, inventory and availability
  • Shipping destinations, costs, timing, returns and contact details
  • Privacy, terms, consent language, and account policies
  • Owner approval for paid services and live payments
